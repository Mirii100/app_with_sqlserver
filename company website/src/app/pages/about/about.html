Who We Are

Empowering communities through responsible finance

Alexia Financials is a premier, member-owned financial institution dedicated to innovative, accessible and secure banking. We believe financial empowerment is the cornerstone of community growth.

From salary accounts and personal loans to chama collective investment and diaspora banking, we combine the discipline of a regulated institution with the purpose of a cooperative — putting member returns first.
